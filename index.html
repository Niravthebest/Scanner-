How the setups are defined

Gap-up / EP
Opens at least the minimum gap above yesterday’s close on at least the minimum relative volume, and closes in the upper half of the day’s range. Stop: the day’s low. When trading live, use the low of the first 5–15 minutes (the opening range) instead.
Pullback
Price is above a rising 21 EMA, which is above the 50 EMA. The stock ran up by at least the minimum prior run, is now at least the minimum pullback below its high, and today’s low tagged or flushed through the 9/21/50 EMA or the anchored VWAP (anchored at the 60-day swing low) and closed back above it. Stop: below the support level or the day’s low, whichever is lower.
Breakout
Closes above a tight base (the last N days within the maximum range) on above-average volume, while above the 50 EMA. Stop: the day’s low.
Sizing
Shares = (account × risk %) ÷ (entry − stop), capped by the maximum position size. Setups whose stop is wider than the maximum stop are flagged wide. Luk skips those or waits for a tighter entry.

Educational tool, not investment advice. Scans use daily bars, so the stops are approximations of intraday levels. Most trades from this style of trading lose; it only works when losses stay small and a few winners run. Paper trade it first.
